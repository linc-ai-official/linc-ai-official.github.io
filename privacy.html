Linc AI — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Hi! 👋 Linc AI is a free, non-commercial, kid-friendly chatbot made as a learning project by a young student. This page explains, in plain language, what information Linc uses and how it's kept safe. We try to collect as little as possible.

What Linc collects

  • Your name and email — only if you choose to sign in (by typing them, or with "Sign in with Google"). We use these to greet you, save your chats on your device, and show your nickname on the game leaderboard.
  • Your chat messages — what you type is sent to our AI service so Linc can write an answer back. Messages aren't used to identify you.
  • A random device code — a little random ID stored in your browser so Linc remembers your settings and chat time on that device.

"Sign in with Google"

When you sign in with Google, Linc only receives your name and email address — nothing else. Linc never sees your Google password; Google handles all of that securely.

Where your information is stored

Most of your things — your chats, settings, and name — are saved only on your own device, inside your browser. A small list of nicknames and game scores is kept on our server just so the leaderboard works and no two people pick the same name.

What Linc does NOT do

  • We do not sell your information to anyone.
  • We do not share it with advertisers.
  • We never collect your passwords.

Deleting your information

You can sign out anytime, and you can clear your browser's data for this site to remove everything saved on your device. If you'd like your nickname removed from the leaderboard, just email us (below).

Linc is built to be safe and friendly for kids. If you're a parent or guardian with any questions or requests, please reach out using the email below. 💚

Changes to this policy

We might update this page from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top will always show the latest version.
